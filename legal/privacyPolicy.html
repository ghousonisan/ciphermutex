Privacy Policy

At Cipher MuteX (“Cipher MuteX”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), your privacy matters. We are committed to protecting the personal information of our clients, partners, and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.


This Policy applies only to information collected online through our website and services. It does not apply to information collected offline or through channels other than our website.

1. Consent

By using our website, you agree to this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection and use of your information as described here.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal details: name, email address, phone number, company name, and address.
  • Communication details: any messages, inquiries, or attachments you share with us.
  • Account details: when you register, we may ask for contact and business information.
  • Technical details: IP address, browser type, ISP, device details, and pages visited (via log files and analytics).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve our website and services.
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests.
  • Send updates, promotional content, or service-related communications (with your consent).
  • Analyze website usage and trends to improve user experience.

4. Log Files

Like most websites, Cipher MuteX uses log files to track visits. These files may collect IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click activity. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information and is used for analytics, security, and site management.

5. Cookies & Web Beacons

We use cookies to enhance your experience, such as remembering preferences and analyzing website activity.

  • You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • Some third-party tools we use (like Google Ads or Analytics) may also use cookies.

6. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google may use cookies (including DART cookies) to serve ads to visitors based on their activity on our site and other websites. You can opt out of DART cookies by visiting Google's Ads Privacy Policy:Google Ad Policy.

7. Advertising & Third-Party Privacy Policies

Some partners or advertisers may use cookies, JavaScript, or tracking technologies. They automatically receive your IP address when this happens.


Cipher MuteX has no control over third-party cookies. We encourage you to review the Privacy Policies of these third parties for more details and opt-out options.

8. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections or updates to your information.
  • Request deletion of your data (under certain conditions).
  • Restrict or object to how we process your data.
  • Request a copy of your data for transfer elsewhere.

We will respond to any valid request within one month. To exercise your rights, please contact us at contact@ciphermutex.com.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has shared such information with us, please contact us immediately, and we will remove it from our records.

10. Updates to This Policy

Cipher MuteX may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
